## Jawaban akhir (ringkas)

Great leaders in today's research are often seen as those who combine authenticity, empathy, and vision with practicality and adaptability. Think of it like the perfect recipe for leadership, where each ingredient plays its role but doesn't overpower the others.

Firstly, **authenticity**—great leaders are genuine and true to themselves. They don’t pretend to be something they’re not. Research shows that authenticity is a key factor in building trust among team members and stakeholders.

Secondly, **empathy**—leaders who understand and share the feelings of their team members tend to foster a more positive work environment. Empathetic leaders are better at motivating and retaining talent by addressing individual needs and concerns.

Thirdly, **visionary thinking**—great leaders have clear, long-term goals that inspire and align their teams towards shared objectives. This doesn’t mean they ignore the present; instead, they balance short-term realities with future aspirations.

Fourthly, **practicality and adaptability**—in an ever-changing world, leaders who can implement strategies effectively while being flexible enough to adjust when necessary are highly valued. They’re like a ship’s captain who knows how to navigate both calm seas and stormy weather.

Lastly, great leaders often embody the concept of **servant leadership**. This means they prioritize the growth and well-being of their team members over their own interests. By serving others first, these leaders create a culture where everyone feels valued and supported.

In summary, modern research suggests that effective leadership is a blend of authenticity, empathy, visionary thinking, practicality, adaptability, and servant leadership. It’s not just about being in charge; it's about leading with heart, mind, and action.

[FAKTA] According to studies by researchers like Daniel Goleman and Simon Sinek, these traits have been consistently linked to successful leadership outcomes.
