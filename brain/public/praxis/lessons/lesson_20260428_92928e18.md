## Jawaban akhir (ringkas)

[NP] Hey there! Let's dive into the exciting world of compound interest and see how it can work its magic over time, shall we? 🚀

Imagine you have a magical piggy bank that doubles your money every year. Now, instead of just doubling what’s inside, our magical piggy bank also adds an extra bonus based on whatever amount is already in there each year. That's compound interest in action! 💰✨

Let's make it more concrete with some numbers:

Suppose you put $100 into your magical piggy bank at the start of Year 1.

- **Year 1**: The initial amount ($100) grows by a fixed rate, say 10%. So, at the end of Year 1, you have $110.

- **Year 2**: Now, not only do you get 10% on your original $100 (that's another $10), but also 10% on the additional $10 that was added last year. So, at the end of Year 2, you have $121.

- **Year 3**: The fun continues! You get 10% on your initial $100 ($10) and another 10% on the $10 from last year (another $1), plus 10% on the extra $1 that was added in Year 2 ($0.10). That’s a total of $133.10 by the end of Year 3.

As you can see, each year, not only are you earning interest on your initial amount but also on all the previously earned interest. This is what makes compound interest so powerful over time! 🌟

So, if you ever find yourself with a bit of extra cash and some time to let it grow, consider letting it work its magic through compound interest. Happy saving!

[FAKTA] Compound interest is essentially earning or paying interest on an amount that includes previous interest payments. This leads to exponential growth over time as the interest earned also starts generating additional interest.
