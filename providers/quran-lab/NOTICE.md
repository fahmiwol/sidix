The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers this package's code and the Lab-authored data
and documentation. It does **not** cover quotations from third-party editions.

Quoted passages from **Terjemah Kemenag RI**, **Tafsir Kemenag RI**,
**Tafsir al-Jalalayn**, and any other edition remain the property of their
publishers. They are included with attribution for commentary and are **not
covered by MIT**. The MIT license does not grant rights over those passages.

Terjemah Kemenag RI and Tafsir Kemenag RI are attributed to Kementerian Agama
Republik Indonesia. Tafsir al-Jalalayn is attributed to Jalal al-Din al-Mahalli
and Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti; rights in a particular translation or edition remain
with its publishers. The local comparison field `jalalain_id` does not identify
an Indonesian translator or publisher, and `tr.en` does not identify its English
edition. No more specific edition attribution is inferred here.

Each study retains its `sourced_meaning.sources` edition and verse references.
[QUOTATIONS_REPORT.md](QUOTATIONS_REPORT.md) records exact-match locations and
counts against the available Indonesian/English translation and tafsir fields.
It contains no quoted text and is not a complete rights inventory: matches under
eight words and material from other editions are outside its detection scope.

No Arabic Qur'an text is bundled. Obtain it from its publishers, for example
Tanzil with attribution or KFGQPC, subject to their own terms. The copied
taxonomy includes individual Arabic cognitive terms; it is not a mushaf or a
collection of Arabic verses. Complete translations, tafsir files and the source
application's archive are not bundled.

The `license: "MIT"` in a provider citation identifies the Lab's authored
interpretation. It does not relicense third-party passages quoted within it.
